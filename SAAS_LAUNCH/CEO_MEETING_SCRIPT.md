**Say:**

## Handle Common Questions

**Q: We already have Tally / SAP**
> "FlowNex360 doesn't replace Tally for accounts — it fills the gap
> Tally can't fill: real-time production floor tracking.
> We can actually export data to Tally format. They work together."

**Q: How long does setup take?**
> "3 weeks. Week 1 we set up the server and configure your machines,
> products, and employees. Week 2 we train your team.
> Week 3 you go live. We stay with you for the first 30 days."

**Q: What if the internet goes down?**
> "FlowNex360 has a LOCAL mode — it runs on your factory's own server.
> Production tracking continues even with zero internet.
> When the connection restores, everything syncs automatically."

**Q: Is my data safe?**
> "Your data is on your server — not on some foreign cloud.
> You own your data completely. We just build and maintain the software."

**Q: Can you customize it for us?**
> "Yes, that's part of what we do. The core system is ready.
> Any specific reports, workflows, or integrations you need —
> we quote separately. Most customizations take 1-2 weeks."

**Q: What about training our workers?**
> "The operator interface is extremely simple — 3-4 fields to fill.
> We've trained factory floor workers in 2 hours.
> For supervisors and managers, one full day of training covers everything."
