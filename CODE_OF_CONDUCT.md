# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are a professional engineering team building and operating a production system. We commit to a
respectful, inclusive, and harassment-free environment for everyone who contributes, regardless of
experience level, background, or role.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive — especially in code review. Critique the code, not the person.
- Assume good intent; ask questions before assigning blame.
- Welcome and mentor junior developers; explain the "why".
- Give and accept feedback gracefully.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and focus on the fix and prevention, not fault.
- Keep production safety and data integrity above ego or speed.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Demeaning, insulting, or dismissive comments.
- Publishing others' private information without consent.
- Sharing customer data, credentials, or secrets outside authorized channels.
- Sustained disruption of discussions or reviews.

## Scope

Applies to all project spaces — repository, PRs, issues, Jira, chat, meetings, and any venue where
someone represents the project.

## Reporting

Report concerns privately to the repository owner / engineering lead (`@malisanjay475`). Reports are
handled confidentially and reviewed promptly. Maintainers who do not uphold this code may face
temporary or permanent consequences at the leadership's discretion.
